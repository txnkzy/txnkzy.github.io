Pitch detection runs in this tab · nothing is uploaded

Bring a recording you have the rights to use. Homerow listens for one melody line, writes it down as notes, and fits those notes onto the nine home-row keys so you can watch it in Listen or practise it in Learn.

Works best on one clear melody. A solo instrument, humming, whistling or unaccompanied singing transcribes cleanly. Full mixes with drums, chords or heavy reverb give a rougher sketch — expect extra, missing or octave-jumping notes.

Links from YouTube, Spotify and other streaming services can't be imported: web pages can't read their audio, and saving it would break their terms of use. Use a file you recorded or own.
